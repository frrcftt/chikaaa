chapter 08 · something I meanplease read this

A little reminder, from my heart

Please be
careful with your heart.

Chika, one thing I really want you to understand is this: don't give everyone the same access to your heart just because you know how to care about people. I know you can trust someone, get attached, tell them things, and genuinely expect them to understand you. But not everyone knows how to value that trust.

Sometimes the people we trust the most are the ones who end up making us cry. And when that happens, please don't start thinking that there's something wrong with you. Your kindness isn't a weakness, your feelings aren't a problem, and caring deeply doesn't make you foolish. It only means your heart is genuine.

But please, don't keep giving chances to people who repeatedly hurt you. Don't ignore your own feelings just to keep someone in your life. Learn to notice who respects you, who listens when you're upset, and who stays kind even when they don't need anything from you. Trust people slowly. Let their actions earn it.

And don't change the good parts of yourself because someone failed to appreciate them. Be yourself, keep growing, and improve for your own happiness—not to prove your worth to anyone. You are already a nice person; just become wiser about who gets to see every side of you.

Stay soft, but don't let people treat you carelessly. Be kind, but keep your boundaries. And whenever life feels heavy, remember that you deserve the same care you keep giving to everyone else.
